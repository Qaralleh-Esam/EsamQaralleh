Courses

Selected courses and teaching areas from my academic experience. Individual pages provide a brief overview of each subject.

Computer Architecture and Organization →

Processor organization, instruction sets, datapaths, memory hierarchy, pipelining, I/O and performance evaluation. Practical activities connect architectural principles with hardware implementation and verification.

Advanced Computer Architecture →

Advanced processor architectures, parallelism, memory systems and architectural performance. Students analyze design trade-offs and evaluate modern computer systems.

Multicore Computing →

Multicore and parallel architectures, thread-level parallelism, shared-memory systems, synchronization, performance optimization and practical parallel programming.

Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning →

Foundations and applications of artificial intelligence, machine learning and deep learning, with attention to engineering applications in intelligent sensing, image analysis, embedded systems and edge computing.

Digital Logic Design →

Number systems, Boolean algebra, logic minimization, combinational circuits, sequential logic, finite-state machines and practical digital design.

Microprocessors and Assembly Language →

Microprocessor organization, instruction sets, assembly-language programming, memory addressing, stack operations, interfacing and practical hardware/software projects.

Embedded Systems →

Microcontroller-based system design, with emphasis on hardware–software integration, I/O, timers, interrupts, ADC, PWM, serial communication, sensors and actuators. Practical work includes embedded programming, simulation and prototyping.

Computer Arithmetic →

Representation and arithmetic of digital numbers, arithmetic circuits and algorithms, and the implementation/performance trade-offs of hardware arithmetic units.

Operating Systems →

Operating-system principles, processes and threads, scheduling, synchronization, memory management and I/O. Laboratory exercises reinforce core operating-system concepts.

Electronics and Digital Electronics →

Fundamentals of electronic devices and circuits, digital electronic building blocks and their role in computer and embedded systems.

Electric Circuits →

Fundamental circuit analysis, voltage and current laws, circuit theorems, and analytical techniques for electrical networks.

Workshop Lab and Engineering Design →

An introductory project-based course in design thinking, problem identification, ideation, prototyping, testing, teamwork and engineering innovation.

Practical Training / Work Placement →

Supervised engineering workplace experience supported by training plans, weekly progress reports, employer feedback and reflective assessment.

Descriptions are concise website summaries, not official university catalog descriptions. The CV also lists additional subjects I am able to teach; those are not presented here as previously taught courses.
