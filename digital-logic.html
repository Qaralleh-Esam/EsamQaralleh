Digital Logic Design & Laboratory

Foundational course covering binary numbers, logic gates, Boolean simplification, combinational circuits, and finite state machines.

Key Topics

  • Number systems, binary arithmetic, and 2's complement representation.
  • Boolean algebra, Karnaugh maps, and logic gate minimization.
  • Multiplexers, decoders, adders, and arithmetic circuits.
  • Flip-flops, counters, shift registers, and Finite State Machine (FSM) design.

ChapterSummary
Chapter 1Binary Systems — Digital systems, binary numbers, number-base conversions, octal and hexadecimal, complements, signed binary numbers, binary codes and binary logic.
Chapter 2Boolean Algebra & Logic Gates — Postulates and theorems of Boolean algebra, Boolean functions, canonical and standard forms, and digital logic gates.
Chapter 3Gate-Level Minimization — Karnaugh-map method, product-of-sums simplification, don't-care conditions, and NAND/NOR implementations.
Chapter 4Combinational Logic — Analysis and design procedures, adders and subtractors, magnitude comparators, decoders, encoders, and multiplexers.
Chapter 5Synchronous Sequential Logic — Latches and flip-flops, analysis of clocked sequential circuits, state reduction and assignment, and design procedure.
Chapter 6Registers & Counters — Registers, shift registers, ripple counters, synchronous counters, and other counter designs.
Chapter 7Memory & Programmable Logic — Random-access memory, memory decoding, ROM, and programmable logic devices (PLA, PAL).
