About Me

I hold a Ph.D. in Electronics from National Chiao Tung University (NCTU), Taiwan, where I was promoted directly from Master's to Ph.D. after ranking among the top 10% of my cohort. Since joining Princess Sumaya University for Technology (PSUT) in 2006, I have dedicated nearly two decades to teaching, research, and academic leadership.

My career spans academic administration as Dean (2021–2023) and Vice Dean (2019–2021) of the School of Engineering, and Chair of the Computer Engineering Department (2010–2012, 2016–2017). I have successfully led ABET accreditation efforts and modernized engineering programs to align with international standards.

Industrial & Academic Synergy: Beyond academia, I bring hands-on industrial experience in computer systems maintenance and VLSI design & verification from Jordan and Taiwan. As an active IEEE Senior Member, I regularly organize and participate in conferences and technical communities.

My research interests have evolved from video coding and embedded systems to encompass robotics, intelligent systems, IoT, AI, and machine learning. I am committed to lifelong learning, continuously integrating emerging technologies into my teaching and research.

Teaching Philosophy

Teaching is both an intellectual responsibility and a creative endeavor. I design meaningful learning experiences that connect knowledge to real understanding, guiding students from what they know toward discovering new concepts through reasoning and exploration.

Project-Based Learning

Students apply theoretical concepts through practical projects using EMU8088, Proteus, logic simulators, and FPGA implementations. Real-world applications transform abstract ideas into tangible skills.

Design Thinking

Integrating empathize, define, ideate, prototype, and test stages into freshmen curriculum to build creativity, problem-solving, and entrepreneurial mindset for the Fourth Industrial Revolution.

Theory-Practice Bridge

Using real-world illustrations and intuitive reasoning to make complex topics accessible. I emphasize conceptual understanding over memorization, encouraging students to explore the logic behind each concept.

Lifelong Learning

I model continuous growth through self-learning in AI, ML, cybersecurity, and IoT. Students are inspired to question existing ideas, explore alternatives, and approach future challenges with innovation.

Research Interests

Video Coding & Compression

Efficient algorithms for H.264/AVC and HEVC

My doctoral work focused on motion estimation for MPEG-4 and H.264 video coding. I continue to develop hardware-efficient block matching units and early termination mechanisms for real-time video compression.

  • Fast variable block-size motion estimation
  • Hardware-efficient H.265/HEVC engines
  • Bit-shrinking architectures for VLSI
  • MATLAB/Simulink verification environments

Artificial Intelligence & Deep Learning

Neural networks for real-world applications

From handwritten Arabic word recognition to medical image analysis, I apply deep learning to solve practical problems in smart cities and healthcare.

  • Handwritten Arabic text recognition (RNNs)
  • Diabetic retinopathy classification
  • Human behavior classification for surveillance
  • License plate recognition systems

IoT & Embedded Systems

Intelligent connected devices and edge computing

Combining hardware design expertise with software intelligence to build practical connected systems for industrial and consumer applications.

  • Hardware-assisted RTOS on FPGA
  • FreeRTOS porting to ARM Cortex-M3
  • Smart city Wi-Fi Direct communication
  • Industrial IoT and edge AI

Cybersecurity & Intelligent Systems

Securing cyber-physical systems

Developing machine learning-enabled intrusion detection systems and secure communication protocols for modern embedded environments.

  • ML-enabled intrusion detection in CPS
  • Symmetric cryptography implementations
  • Secure embedded system design
  • Driving event classification using mobile sensors

Robotics & Automation
