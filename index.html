About Me

Dr. Esam A. Al-Qaralleh holds the position of Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering within the Computer Engineering Department at the King Abdullah II School of Engineering, Princess Sumaya University for Technology (PSUT) in Amman, Jordan. He became part of the PSUT community in June 2006 and has since been a dedicated educator and researcher.

Over the years, he has delivered a wide range of courses for graduate and undergraduate students and contributed to the field with over 30 peer-reviewed scientific publications. His commitment to the academic community extends far beyond the classroom — he has taken on key roles in organizing numerous IEEE conferences, both locally and regionally.

Leadership & Accreditation

As an active senior IEEE member, Dr. Qaralleh has played a crucial role in coordinating various IEEE events in Jordan. He has served on accreditation advisory boards, international conference scientific committees, technical program committees, and organizational committees. He led the School of Engineering to attain ABET EAC accreditation for the Networks & Information Security Engineering Bachelor Program and maintained accreditation for all other programs.

Dr. Qaralleh brings a wealth of industrial experience, having worked in computer engineering at various institutions in Jordan and Taiwan prior to and throughout his academic career. His industrial background includes roles as a Computer Systems Maintenance Engineer at Euro-Gulf Electronics Co. and a Maintenance Engineer at the Royal Scientific Society, as well as part-time work as a Testing and Evaluation Engineer at the Industrial Technology Research Institute in Hsin-Chu, Taiwan.

Throughout his academic journey, he has accumulated extensive experience in curriculum design and pedagogy in higher education and has led several accreditation committees to achieve national and international recognition. His leadership extends to senior positions such as Dean and Vice-Dean of the School of Engineering and Department Chair.

Mentorship & Student Development

Dr. Qaralleh's dedication to nurturing talent is evident through his mentorship of numerous student scientific clubs, which have actively participated in regional and international competitions. Many of his senior design project students have received recognition and awards in local and regional competitions.
