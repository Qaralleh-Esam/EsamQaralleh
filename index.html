Biography

Dr. Esam A. Al-Qaralleh is a Professor of Computer Engineering in the King Abdullah II School of Engineering at Princess Sumaya University for Technology (PSUT), Amman, Jordan. He joined PSUT in 2006 and teaches undergraduate and graduate courses in computer and electrical engineering. His research interests include artificial intelligence, intelligent embedded systems, FPGA-based computing, computer architecture, video and image processing, and hardware–software co-design.

He has served as Dean and Vice Dean of the School of Engineering and Head of the Computer Engineering Department. He has contributed to curriculum development, academic quality assurance and ABET accreditation, with a particular interest in practical and project-based engineering education. Before joining PSUT, he worked in engineering and research roles in Jordan and Taiwan. He remains involved in IEEE activities, research collaborations and student mentorship.
