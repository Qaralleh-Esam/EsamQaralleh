Biography

Dr. Esam A. AlQaralleh, Ph.D., is a Professor of Computer Engineering at the King Abdullah II School of Engineering (KASE), Princess Sumaya University for Technology (PSUT), Amman, Jordan. He joined PSUT in 2006 and has over 20 years of teaching and academic experience in Computer and Electrical Engineering at both undergraduate and graduate levels.

His teaching and research interests include Artificial Intelligence, deep learning, intelligent embedded systems, IoT, FPGA-based computing, video and image processing, computer architecture, and hardware-software co-design. He has extensive leadership experience having served as Dean, Vice Dean, and Head of the Computer Engineering Department at PSUT.
